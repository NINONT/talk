Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

T.A.L.K. is a Windows app made by QuadRival ("we"). This policy explains what information the app and this website handle, and where it goes. In short: T.A.L.K. runs on your PC, we don't run servers that receive your stream or your chat, and we don't collect analytics.

What stays on your PC

Your settings, your character, your API keys, your shoutout list and clips, and the voice audio T.A.L.K. saves are stored only in the T.A.L.K. folder on your PC. We never receive them. Uninstalling T.A.L.K. leaves your settings in place in case you reinstall; you can delete the folder at any time.

What T.A.L.K. sends, and to whom

To work, the app talks directly from your PC to services you choose and set up yourself:

  • Your AI service (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude): chat messages and viewer names that the co-host replies to, along with your character description, so the AI can write the reply.
  • Your voice service, if you use an online one (ElevenLabs or Microsoft's online voices): the text being spoken. The offline voices send nothing.
  • TikTok, Twitch and YouTube: T.A.L.K. reads your public live chat. For YouTube it uses your own Google API key. To check whether your Twitch channel is live, it asks decapi.me, a public service, using your channel name.
  • Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider: your license key and a label for your PC, so it can confirm your subscription and make sure each key is used on one PC at a time.
  • GitHub: when T.A.L.K. checks for updates, it asks GitHub for the latest version number.

Each of these services handles that information under its own privacy policy. We don't receive copies.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the reseller (merchant of record). Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email, billing details and payment information, and shares with us what we need to support you, such as your name, email and order status. We never see your full card number.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to help you. We don't send marketing email unless you ask to hear from us.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, they may log technical information such as your IP address. We don't use cookies, analytics or ads on this site.

Children

T.A.L.K. is meant for streamers who are allowed to use TikTok, Twitch or YouTube LIVE features, and it isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can stop T.A.L.K. from sending anything by stopping the bot, turning a platform off in Settings, or uninstalling it. To ask about the information Lemon Squeezy holds about a purchase, or anything in this policy, email quadrivaltv@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, mention it in the app's update notes.
