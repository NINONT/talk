Refund policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

You can try every feature of T.A.L.K. free for 30 days before paying anything, so you know what you're getting. If it still isn't right for you after you subscribe, here's how refunds work.

First payment

If you're not happy, email quadrivaltv@gmail.com within 14 days of your first payment and we'll refund it in full. Monthly or yearly, no hoops.

Renewals

Renewal payments aren't refunded, but you can cancel anytime so you aren't charged again, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. If you forgot to cancel a yearly renewal, email us within 7 days of the renewal and we'll refund it.

Problems we can't fix

If T.A.L.K. stops working for you because of a problem on our side that we can't fix, email us and we'll refund the current period.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider. They usually show up within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Charges from other services

Charges from Google, Anthropic, ElevenLabs or other services you use with T.A.L.K. are billed by those companies, so we can't refund them. Contact them directly.
